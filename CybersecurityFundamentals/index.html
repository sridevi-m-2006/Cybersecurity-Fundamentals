Introduction to Cybersecurity

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, applications and data from cyber attacks.

Ethical hacking helps identify security weaknesses with proper permission.

Types of Hackers

White Hat Hacker

Ethical hackers who find security weaknesses legally and help protect systems.

Black Hat Hacker

Hackers who access systems illegally and use their skills for harmful purposes.

Grey Hat Hacker

Hackers who may find vulnerabilities without permission but do not always have harmful intentions.

CIA Triad

The CIA Triad is the foundation of information security.

Confidentiality

Ensures that information is accessible only to authorized people.

Integrity

Ensures that data remains accurate and is not changed without authorization.

Availability

Ensures that systems and information are available when needed.

Common Cybersecurity Threats

Malware

Malicious software designed to damage or access computer systems.

Phishing

A technique used to trick people into sharing passwords or personal information through fake messages or websites.

Ransomware

A type of malware that locks or encrypts files and demands money to restore access.
